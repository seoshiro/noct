## Direction and acceptance gates

Smoke-grey cyclorama, broad white strip reflections, deep neutral black lacquer, brushed/polished warm gold, soft contact shadow. One centered object with generous space. Large restrained serif title and legible dark interface type. Product choices and cart follow the narrative.

Story progress is continuous and reversible: front view → restrained 20° turn → crown lifts clear of the nozzle → head depresses → one directional spray → geranium → tonka bean → sandalwood → cloud dissolves and the object returns for selection. Particle trajectories, three stable anchor drops, camera and HTML/SVG callouts share the same progress. No wheel hijacking and no elapsed-time cloud drift while reading. Mobile keeps the bottle and active note together. Reduced motion and WebGL fallback provide sequential readable notes.

First gate: convincing still browser render, front / three-quarter / crown macro, before adding story animation. Inspect crown negative spaces and real metal thickness, long lacquer reflections, readable edges and front marking. Then animate one scent fully; connect RAIN/VEIL and retained cart/samples afterwards.
